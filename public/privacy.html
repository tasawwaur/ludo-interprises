Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 08, 2026

Welcome to Ludo Legends ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and security of your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic profile information when you register or sign in through Google, Facebook, or as a Guest. This includes your name, profile picture, and in-game statistics (XP, matches played, coins, and levels).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your details are used solely to run multiplayer matchmaking matches, sync friend lists, track leaderboard rankings, and verify transaction receipts.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal data with third-party advertisers. All transaction histories and currency logs are secured with standard encryption.

4. Data Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your account and related data, please contact our support team at support@ludolegends.com.
